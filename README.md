# Ampulse

*ampoule + pulse* — an offline phone app for tracking pills, injections and blood pressure: for
yourself, a family member or a pet. [Русский](README.ru.md)

**Status:** design mockup (49 screens, Russian and English, light and dark theme) and the 0.2.0 app
skeleton for Android (tabs with empty states, settings, update from GitHub). The APK will be in
[Releases](https://github.com/AgitAngst/Ampulse/releases) with the first release.

## Ideas

- **Fully offline.** Prescriptions are recognized on the phone: rules, an on-device medicine directory,
  photo and speech recognition. No cloud.
- **Prescription → plan.** Paste text, take a photo or dictate — the app lays the course out by your
  daily routine and asks about what is missing.
- **Safety first.** Double-dose guard, a step-by-step injection guide with a countdown, an emergency
  card on the lock screen. The app never changes doses.
- **Family without a cloud.** Missed-dose texts, full data handover by QR when you are together, a PDF
  report for the doctor.
- **More:** injection site rotation, supply and expiry tracking, blood pressure with its own keypad,
  pets.
